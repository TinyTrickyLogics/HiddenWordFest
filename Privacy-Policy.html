02

Game & gameplay

Hidden Word Fest is a word-search puzzle game in which players search for hidden, semi-transparent alphabets placed across interactive game boards. Players tap hidden letters to complete required words and clear levels.

🔎 Hidden lettersSearch and identify letters within each level.
👆 Touch controlsTap, zoom, and pan around the level canvas.
⏱️ Time challengesComplete puzzles within the available time.
💡 Helpful optionsUse hints or revive/add-time features where available.

These core gameplay interactions do not require players to submit private details such as their name, physical address, or phone number to our servers.
